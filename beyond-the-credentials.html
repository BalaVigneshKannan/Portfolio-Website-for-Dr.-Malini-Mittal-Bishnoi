Research · Development · Academia

Dr. Malini Mittal Bishnoi

Dr. Malini Mittal Bishnoi is an academician and development professional whose work sits at the intersection of curriculum design, research strategy, and pedagogical innovation.

Ph.D.

Sociology — Delhi School of Economics, University of Delhi

18+ Years

University teaching across AUK and GUST — the Social, Behavioral and Allied Sciences

My career bridges academia and the development sector. I have worked with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in India, taken part in a professional exchange with Women and Rural Economic Development (WRED) Canada, and led corporate-foundation initiatives on adolescent mental health and well-being. I have also contributed to consultancy and research assignments with the Department of Education, Delhi University.

My Journey

Curiosity anchors everything Dr. Malini does. My perspective on human development keeps evolving, sharpened by two decades spent moving between grassroots development work and the academy.

  1. 1

    As Project Officer with the United Nations Development Programme, I monitored projects and ensured their effective rollout across several Indian states — an intergovernmental balancing act that meant coordinating with government ministries, private consultancies such as McKinsey, and a wide network of NGOs and civil society organizations.

  2. 2

    These multi-stakeholder collaborations, which fed directly into the Human Development Report, gave me a grounded, field-tested understanding of how policy meets people.

  3. 3

    That grounding runs through my scholarship today. Sociology, social work, social psychology, and research spanning education, migration, gender, and culture converge in my writing and public discourse.

  4. 4

    My recent book, Migrant Memoirs from the Middle East, distills fieldwork among Kuwait's pink-collar workers into a compelling, human account of migration and labor in the Gulf.
